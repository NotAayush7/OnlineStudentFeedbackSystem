Guidelines for Providing Feedback

Before you submit your feedback, please keep the following points in mind:

  • Be specific and provide examples where possible.
  • Constructive feedback helps improve teaching methods and course design.
  • All responses remain confidential — your identity will not be disclosed.

The data you submit will be securely stored and analyzed by the administration for academic quality assurance.
