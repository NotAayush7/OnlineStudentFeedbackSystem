Why Provide Feedback?

Your feedback helps faculty understand what works well in a course and what could be improved. Constructive responses help create better study materials, clearer instruction, and a more engaging classroom experience.

  • Every response remains confidential.
  • Feedback is analyzed for continuous improvement.
  • Your participation contributes to better learning outcomes for everyone.
